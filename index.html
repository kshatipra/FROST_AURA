Straight answers.

Does it work with my bottle?

Yes. Aura is designed for the bottle, tumbler or mug you already own. No special bottle needed.

How does Aura know when to glow?

Aura senses your bottle on the dock and times its light cues around your day.

Do I need my phone?

No. The cues happen on your desk, in light. The web app is there if you want to see your day.

What works today, and what's coming?

Sip and move cues are the heart of Aura. Focus sessions and breathing cues are planned before mass production.

When does it ship?

Indiegogo's current estimate is December 2026 to February 2027. Crowdfunding timelines can change, and we'll post updates on the campaign page.

Is Aura a medical device?

No. It's a reminder light for your desk, not a medical product.

Can I cancel my pledge?

Yes, before the campaign ends. See the refund policy on Indiegogo.
